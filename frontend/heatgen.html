Heat generation

Steps

  1. Choose the meet

    Pick the meet you're drawing heats for.

  2. Add swimmers and races

    Import a spreadsheet, or add swimmers on the Player list.

  3. Lock the player list

    Locking stops new entries, so the heats won't change after you draw them.

    Open the player list

  4. Generate heats

    Check the pools, then generate. Nothing is saved yet.

  5. Check the lanes and save

    Swap lanes if needed, then save. Saved heats go to Race control and the public Heat sheets.

  6. Run the races

    Call heats poolside and enter times as each heat finishes.

    Open race control Live results
