Strict Non-Medalist Eligibility Guidelines

INELIGIBLE: Any swimmer who has won an individual medal (Gold/Silver/Bronze) at State, Zonal, National, or recognized School meets (CBSE National, SGFI, ICSE, DYES).

ALLOWED: Relay medalists, internal club/school non-medalist participants.

AFFILIATION RULE: Represent ONLY ONE institution (School OR Club). Dual representation is blocked.

Registration & Fee Structure

  • Individual Fee: ₹800 for 3 events (Max 3 Individual Events)
  • Relay Add-On: ₹300 per team entry (Billed separately)
  • Swim-Up Policy: SINGLE CATEGORY ONLY (Swim-Up Disabled for this meet)

Mandatory Documents Required at Poolside

  1. Original Birth Certificate or Aadhaar Card for age verification.
  2. Coach / Parent Non-Medalist Signed Undertaking Form.

Rules & Regulation

Rule 1: Age Cutoff Engine

Age is calculated strictly as of December 31st of the competition year. For 2026:

  • U-10: Born 2016–2017
  • U-12: Born 2014–2015
  • U-14: Born 2012–2013
  • U-16: Born 2010–2011

Rule 2: Non-Medalist Enforcement

  • Individual Medalist Exclusion: Swimmers with individual medal records at recognized State, Zonal, National, or School Board meets are barred from entry.
  • Relay Exception: Swimmers who have won medals only in team relay events remain fully eligible.
  • Single Institution Rule: Swimmers are restricted to entering under one entity (either School OR Academy/Club). Dual representation is blocked.

Rule 3: Event Capping

  • Maximum 3 individual events per swimmer.
  • Relay entries are billed as optional add-on fees separate from individual event fees.
  • If both 50m and 100m of the same stroke are selected, they count as 2 separate events toward the 3-event cap.

Rule 4: Swim-Up Policy

This meet enforces Single Category Only. Swim-Up is disabled. Swimmers can register only within their exact auto-derived age group.
